About Boozy Brown Lentil Soup

I’m a home cook, recipe lover, and firm believer that good food doesn’t have to be complicated. I created this space to share the recipes I actually love making—simple, delicious, and made for sharing.

01 I cook because...

Food has a way of bringing people together. I love turning a few simple ingredients into something that makes an ordinary day feel a little more special.

02 My favorite thing to make…

Anything comforting, flavorful, and worth going back for seconds. I’m especially drawn to recipes that are easy enough for a weeknight but still feel a little bit exciting.

03 You’ll find here…

Recipes that are approachable, unfussy, and full of flavor. From quick dinners to weekend treats, everything here is made to be cooked, shared, and enjoyed.

My cookbook is packed with simple, delicious dishes made for everyday cooking and sharing.
